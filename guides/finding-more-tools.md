# Finding More Tools

![](../assets/images/finding-more-tools/finding-more-tools-icon.png) <!-- review-alt -->

These free websites help you find more assistive technology and accessibility settings than this resource covers.

**On this page:**

- [My Computer My Way](#my-computer-my-way)
- [Unified Listing](#unified-listing)
- [AT Hive](#at-hive)

## My Computer My Way

[My Computer My Way](https://mcmw.abilitynet.org.uk), from the UK charity AbilityNet, has step-by-step guides to the accessibility features built into phones, tablets, and computers. You can filter the guides by need, symptom, condition, and device.

My Computer My Way detects your device and operating system and shows the guides for it. To look up a different device, for example when helping someone else, change the **Operating system** filter.
![The My Computer My Way filters with the Operating system menu circled and open, listing All, Android, Android 10, Android 11 and Android 12.](../assets/images/finding-more-tools/mcmw-operating-system-filter.png) <!-- review-alt -->

<details markdown="block">
<summary>Filters</summary>

There are five filter menus.
![The My Computer My Way search box above five filter menus: Category, Adjustment, Condition, Symptom and Operating system, with a Clear all filters link.](../assets/images/finding-more-tools/mcmw-filters.png) <!-- review-alt -->

The first menu is **Category**: Cognitive, Hearing, Motor, and Vision. Each filter menu works with a mouse or keyboard, and you can type to search within it.
![The Category filter open with checkboxes for Cognitive, Hearing, Motor and Vision.](../assets/images/finding-more-tools/mcmw-category-filter.png) <!-- review-alt -->

You can select one or more options in each filter. You don’t need to use every filter, but each one you use narrows down the options in the others.

The next filter, **Adjustment**, includes options such as captioning, colour settings, keyboard settings, magnify, text settings, voice options, zoom and more.
![The Adjustment filter open with checkboxes including Auto correction, Captions, Colour settings, Font settings and Gestures.](../assets/images/finding-more-tools/mcmw-adjustment-filter.png) <!-- review-alt -->

The next menu, **Condition**, lists common physical, cognitive, vision, hearing, and neurological conditions.
![The Condition filter open with checkboxes including ADHD, ALS, Amputation, Anxiety and Arthritis.](../assets/images/finding-more-tools/mcmw-condition-filter.png) <!-- review-alt -->

Use this filter with caution, even if your condition is listed: a useful tool won’t appear if it isn’t tagged with that condition, so you could miss it.

The fourth menu is **Symptom**, such as eye strain, hearing loss, low dexterity, or problems reading.
![The Symptom filter open with checkboxes including Back pain, Blurred vision, Colour blind, Concentration problems and Dizziness.](../assets/images/finding-more-tools/mcmw-symptom-filter.png) <!-- review-alt -->

The last menu is **Operating system**, which is set to your own device to start with. You can choose only one operating system at a time.
![The Operating system filter open, listing All, Android, Android 10, Android 11 and Android 12.](../assets/images/finding-more-tools/mcmw-operating-system-menu.png) <!-- review-alt -->

</details>

<details markdown="block">
<summary>Clear Filters</summary>

To remove a filter, select the **X** beside it. To remove them all, select **Clear all filters**.
![A selected Back pain filter with its X button circled, and the Clear all filters link circled.](../assets/images/finding-more-tools/mcmw-clear-filters.png) <!-- review-alt -->

</details>

<details markdown="block">
<summary>Search</summary>

Search is handy when you know what you’re looking for. To discover features and tools, the filters are usually easier.
![The My Computer My Way search box, reading Start typing to search.](../assets/images/finding-more-tools/mcmw-search.png) <!-- review-alt -->

</details>

<details markdown="block">
<summary>Settings</summary>

My Computer My Way has no settings to change. If the site isn’t working properly, try clearing its cookies and site data in your browser’s settings.

</details>

<details markdown="block">
<summary>Notable Issues</summary>

My Computer My Way doesn’t cover separate assistive technology products such as JAWS, Read&Write, or Kurzweil.

It is generally up to date, covers older operating systems too, and is the most complete collection of guides to built-in accessibility tools and settings. Even so, it doesn’t list everything: there may be useful tools and settings that it doesn’t cover.

</details>

<details markdown="block">
<summary>Links and Further Reading</summary>

## Unified Listing

The [Unified Listing](https://ul.gpii.net) is a worldwide directory of assistive technology and accessible products for communication, computers, and digital devices. It combines more than a dozen databases and is run by Raising the Floor (the makers of [Morphic](morphic.md)). If you create a free account, you can get email updates about particular products.

You can find products in five ways:

- Power Search
- Guided Shopping
- Classic Search
- Find Similar Products
- View Products by Manufacturer

![The Unified Listing search options: Power Search, Classic Search, Guided Shopping and Find Similar Products, with a View Products By Manufacturer button.](../assets/images/finding-more-tools/ul-search-options.png) <!-- review-alt -->

<details markdown="block">
<summary>Power Search</summary>

Power Search combines search terms with filters for difficulties (hearing, seeing, reading, and so on), operating systems, and product types. Add one filter at a time to narrow down the results.
![The Power Search page with Difficulties filters on the left, a search box and results in the middle, and Operating Systems and Product Types filters on the right.](../assets/images/finding-more-tools/ul-power-search.png) <!-- review-alt -->

Each filter has a **Clear** button to reset it.

Above the search field, select **Share or save this search** to get a link to your results, which you can save or share.
![The Power Search box with the tooltip Share or save this search above a link button, next to an email button and a Clear all button.](../assets/images/finding-more-tools/ul-share-search.png) <!-- review-alt -->

</details>

<details markdown="block">
<summary>Guided Shopping</summary>

Guided Shopping groups products by type, such as **Computer Access and Use**, **Person-to-Person Communication**, and **Education**.

Each type leads to more specific questions. When you reach the kind of product you want, you can search the results or filter them by operating system.
![Guided Shopping results for Access to Equations, with a keyword search box, 8 results, and Operating System filters.](../assets/images/finding-more-tools/ul-guided-shopping-results.png) <!-- review-alt -->

Use the breadcrumb links at the top of the results to go back through your choices.
![The breadcrumb trail Guided Shopping, Education, Student tools for homework, Mathematics Aids, Access to Equations, outlined in red.](../assets/images/finding-more-tools/ul-guided-shopping-breadcrumbs.png) <!-- review-alt -->

</details>

<details markdown="block">
<summary>Classic Search</summary>

Classic Search lets you search by keyword or product name and filter by operating system. It is simpler than Power Search.

Above the search field, select **Share or save this search** to get a link to your results.
![The Classic Search box with the share link button circled.](../assets/images/finding-more-tools/ul-classic-search.png) <!-- review-alt -->

</details>

<details markdown="block">
<summary>Find Similar Products</summary>

Start with a product you already know, then choose the features of it that matter to you.

1. Search or scroll through the list of products.
   ![The Find Similar Products page with a box to type in or choose a product name and a Submit button.](../assets/images/finding-more-tools/ul-find-similar-products.png) <!-- review-alt -->
2. Choose a product, then select the features of it that you use.
   ![Find Products Similar to Read&Write, with checkboxes for product category, primary features and operating system.](../assets/images/finding-more-tools/ul-similar-to-read-and-write.png) <!-- review-alt -->
3. Matching products appear below as you select features.
   ![116 results, starting with Read&Write as the starting point, followed by ClaroRead, WordTalk and Orato.](../assets/images/finding-more-tools/ul-similar-results.png) <!-- review-alt -->
   1. Results can be sorted.
4. Select a result to see the guide for that product.
   ![The Unified Listing guide for ClaroRead, describing each edition, with a Notify me of updates button and a list of partner databases.](../assets/images/finding-more-tools/ul-claroread-guide.png) <!-- review-alt -->

</details>

<details markdown="block">
<summary>View Products by Manufacturer</summary>

**View Products by Manufacturer** lists manufacturers alphabetically. Select a letter and then a manufacturer to see all its products. For example, **T > TextHelp** shows all of TextHelp’s products in the Unified Listing.
![Products from this manufacturer: Browsealoud, EquatIO, Fluency Tutor, Read&Write, Snapverter, SpeechStream and WordSmith v2.0.](../assets/images/finding-more-tools/ul-manufacturer-products.png) <!-- review-alt -->

</details>

<details markdown="block">
<summary>Software Guides</summary>

Each product has a guide.
![The Unified Listing guide for Read&Write with a description, main features, primary specifications, options and accessories, and links to more information.](../assets/images/finding-more-tools/ul-read-and-write-guide.png) <!-- review-alt -->

Guides describe the product and its main features, with links to more information.

**Find products similar to this one** opens **Find Similar Products** with that product already selected.
![The Find products similar to this one button.](../assets/images/finding-more-tools/ul-find-similar-button.png) <!-- review-alt -->

</details>

<details markdown="block">
<summary>Settings</summary>

You can change how the Unified Listing looks. At the top of the page, select **+show preferences**.
![The top of the Unified Listing home page with an arrow pointing to the show preferences button.](../assets/images/finding-more-tools/ul-show-preferences.png) <!-- review-alt -->

Scroll sideways through the settings:

- **Text size**: up to twice the default size.
- **Text style**: Times New Roman, Comic Sans, Arial, or Verdana.
- **Line spacing**: up to twice the default spacing.
- **Contrast**: five higher-contrast colour schemes.
- **Table of contents**: adds a table of contents near the top left of the page, to jump to different parts of it.
- **Enhance inputs**: makes links, buttons, menus, text fields, and other controls larger and easier to see.

Click **reset** to set everything back to the defaults.
   ![The preferences panel with text size, text style and line spacing controls, and hide preferences and reset buttons.](../assets/images/finding-more-tools/ul-preferences.png) <!-- review-alt -->

</details>

<details markdown="block">
<summary>Notable Issues</summary>

Even with more than a dozen databases, the Unified Listing doesn’t include every product. Some of its databases may be out of date or no longer maintained, so check details with the manufacturer.

</details>

## AT Hive

The [AT Hive](https://www.ahead.ie/Discover-your-AT), from the Irish education charity AHEAD, helps you find assistive technology by what you want to do, the devices you use, and the accounts you have. For each tool, it has how-to guides, links, advice, and demonstration videos.

The home page has an introductory video on how to use it.
![The Discover your Assistive Technologies page with an introductory video and the Find Assistive Technology button circled.](../assets/images/finding-more-tools/at-hive-home.png) <!-- review-alt -->

<details markdown="block">
<summary>Using the AT Hive</summary>

1. Select **Find Assistive Technology**, then choose what you want help with: reading, magnification, revision and recall, writing, note-taking, spelling & grammar, organisation, motivation and focus, working together, captions and subtitles, recording, or mindset.
   ![Question 1 of 3, What interests would you like to explore today, with options such as Reading, Magnification, Revision and Recall, Writing, Note-taking, and Spelling & Grammar.](../assets/images/finding-more-tools/at-hive-interests.png) <!-- review-alt -->
2. Choose one or more, then select **Next Question** at the bottom of the page.
   ![More interest options, with the Next Question button circled at the bottom.](../assets/images/finding-more-tools/at-hive-next-question.png) <!-- review-alt -->
3. Select the devices you use.
   ![Question 2 of 3, What do you use, with device options including Chrome Book, Microsoft Surface, iPhone, iPad, Apple Computer, Android Phone, Android Tablet and Windows Computer.](../assets/images/finding-more-tools/at-hive-devices.png) <!-- review-alt -->
4. If there is a **Detect My Current Device** button above the options, you can use it to select your current device automatically. Then select **Next Question**.
   ![The rest of the device options, with the Next Question button circled.](../assets/images/finding-more-tools/at-hive-devices-next.png) <!-- review-alt -->
5. Select **Microsoft Account**, **Google Account**, or **No specific account needed**, then **Show My Results**.
   ![Question 3 of 3, with Microsoft Account (Office 365), Google Account and No specific account needed, and the Show My Results button circled.](../assets/images/finding-more-tools/at-hive-accounts.png) <!-- review-alt -->
6. The AT Hive shows the tools that match your answers. Click an answer to remove it. You can also filter by price (free, freemium, free trial, or paid) and email yourself the results.
   ![The AT Matches page showing the chosen answers, pricing filters, and options to email the results or start again.](../assets/images/finding-more-tools/at-hive-matches.png) <!-- review-alt -->
7. Scroll down to the results. Select **Tell me more** for guides, links, and demonstration videos.
   ![Two results, Blackboard Document to MP3 and Caption Ed, each with a Tell me more button and details on device, account, purpose and pricing.](../assets/images/finding-more-tools/at-hive-results.png) <!-- review-alt -->

</details>

<details markdown="block">
<summary>Settings</summary>

The AT Hive has no settings to change. If the site isn’t working properly, try clearing its cookies and site data in your browser’s settings.

</details>

<details markdown="block">
<summary>Notable Issues</summary>

The AT Hive includes only a few of the tools and settings built into devices, and doesn’t list specialist assistive technology such as JAWS, NVDA, or Kurzweil.

</details>
