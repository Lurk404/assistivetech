<details id="word-dictate" markdown="block">
<summary>Word Dictate</summary>

To dictate speech-to-text, use any version (Windows, macOS, Web, mobile) of Microsoft Word:

1. On the **Home** ribbon, click the microphone icon labelled **Dictate**.
   ![The Word Home ribbon with the Dictate button circled.](../assets/images/speech-to-text-tools/word-dictate-button.png) <!-- review-alt -->
2. Wait for the Dictate toolbar and **Listening** message to appear.
   <img src="../assets/images/speech-to-text-tools/word-dictate-toolbar.png" alt="The Dictate toolbar with microphone, settings, help and close buttons." width="390"> <!-- review-alt -->
   1. Ensure you verify the correct microphone is selected in the Settings menu.
      ![The Dictate toolbar with the settings button outlined.](../assets/images/speech-to-text-tools/word-dictate-settings-button.png) <!-- review-alt -->
      ![Dictation Settings with the Microphone menu open, listing three microphones.](../assets/images/speech-to-text-tools/word-dictation-settings-microphone.png) <!-- review-alt -->
3. Speak slowly and clearly to dictate text.
4. Speak punctuation such as “period,” “comma,” and “question mark.”
5. Say “pause dictation” or “stop dictation” to stop dictation. Click the microphone to resume.

[Dictate your documents in Word](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/dictate-your-documents-in-word-3876e05f-3fcc-418f-b8ab-db7ce0d11d3c)

### Settings

![The Dictate toolbar with the settings (gear) button circled.](../assets/images/speech-to-text-tools/word-dictate-gear.png) <!-- review-alt -->

The gear icon on the toolbar opens **Dictation Settings** which includes options for:

1. Spoken language.
   1. Set language for accurate spelling.
2. Microphone.
   1. If multiple microphones are available, this menu indicates which will be used.
3. Auto-punctuation.
   1. Toggle this setting on or off.
4. Filter sensitive phrases.
   1. On by default, this setting ensures any errors in dictation don’t incorrectly attribute potentially offensive phrases to the speaker. Profanity or other sensitive language will be censored with asterisks.

</details>

<details id="windows-voice-typing" markdown="block">
<summary>Windows Voice Typing</summary>

Windows Voice Typing allows users to type using their voice anywhere text can be inputted, including programs and web pages. To use **Voice Typing**:

1. Open **Settings** and navigate to **Privacy & security**. Select **Speech** and toggle **Online speech recognition** on.
2. Place your cursor in any text box and press <kbd>Windows</kbd> + <kbd>H</kbd> to dictate text.
   ![The Voice Typing window with settings, microphone and help buttons.](../assets/images/speech-to-text-tools/windows-voice-typing.png) <!-- review-alt -->
3. Select the cog icon to access **Settings**.
   ![Voice Typing settings with Voice typing launcher and Automatic punctuation switches, Select default microphone, Give feedback and Microsoft Speech Services.](../assets/images/speech-to-text-tools/windows-voice-typing-settings.png) <!-- review-alt -->
   1. Toggle **Voice typing launcher** on to quickly access voice typing whenever you place the cursor in a text box.
      ![The small Voice typing launcher with a microphone button.](../assets/images/speech-to-text-tools/windows-voice-typing-launcher.png) <!-- review-alt -->
4. Voice Typing may display tips and suggestions.
   ![A Voice Typing tip: To move keyboard focus to the voice typing tool, press Windows key + Alt + H.](../assets/images/speech-to-text-tools/windows-voice-typing-tip.png) <!-- review-alt -->

[Use voice typing to talk instead of type on your PC](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/use-voice-typing-to-talk-instead-of-type-on-your-pc-fec94565-c4bd-329d-e59a-af033fa5689f). Take note of the voice typing and punctuation commands to learn shortcuts and useful tips.

[How to use Voice typing in Windows 11 – My Computer My Way](https://mcmw.abilitynet.org.uk/how-to-use-voice-typing-in-windows-11)

</details>

<details id="android" markdown="block">
<summary>Android</summary>

Users can dictate text in Android and ChromeOS. Many default apps (Chrome, Play Store, Docs) will include a microphone icon in the search box. Use that to search using your voice.

<img src="../assets/images/speech-to-text-tools/android-keyboard-microphone.jpg" alt="An Android keyboard with the microphone button circled in the toolbar above the keys." width="300"> <!-- review-alt -->

Tap the microphone to type using your voice.

Voice typing should be available by default, however if you do not see the microphone icon:

1. Navigate to **Settings > General Management**.
2. Select **Keyboard list and default**.
   <img src="../assets/images/speech-to-text-tools/android-keyboard-list.jpg" alt="Android General management settings with Keyboard list and default circled." width="300"> <!-- review-alt -->
3. Toggle on **Google Voice Typing**.
   <img src="../assets/images/speech-to-text-tools/android-google-voice-typing.jpg" alt="Keyboard list and default settings with Google Voice Typing switched on and circled." width="300"> <!-- review-alt -->

Use **Live Transcribe** to transcribe audio. To turn on **Live Transcribe**:

1. Navigate to **Settings > Accessibility**.
2. Select **Installed apps**.
3. Choose **Live Transcribe** and toggle **Live Transcribe shortcut** to **On**.
4. Tap on **Accessibility shortcut**.
5. Select **Live Transcribe**.
   1. Follow prompts to set permissions.
6. Activate Live Transcribe to transcribe audio to text.

[Use Google Assistant to type with your voice](https://support.google.com/gboard/answer/11197787?hl=en)

</details>

### macOS

macOS has a built-in dictation feature to use speech input to write documents. To enable:

1. Navigate to **System Settings**.
2. Select **Keyboard**.
3. Select the **Dictation** tab.
4. Toggle the button on.
   ![macOS System Settings with an arrow pointing to Keyboard in the sidebar and the Dictation switch circled. The Shortcut setting is underlined.](../assets/images/speech-to-text-tools/macos-dictation-setting.png) <!-- review-alt -->

To dictate text, select a text edit field (such as the browser address bar or a document) and activate Dictation using the listed shortcut (the microphone key or <kbd>F5</kbd> by default) or navigate to **Edit > Start Dictation**.

Speak slowly and articulate as best you can. Use natural language for commands, such as “new line” or “exclamation point”.

macOS has a [complete list of dictation commands](https://support.apple.com/en-ca/guide/mac-help/mh40695/12.0/mac/12.0).

Learn more about how to [dictate messages and documents](https://support.apple.com/en-ca/guide/mac-help/mh40584/mac) on Mac.

Under **System Preferences > Keyboard** take note of language for spelling purposes. Verify correct microphone source. Choose between shortcuts or customize.

<details id="dragon" markdown="block">
<summary>Dragon</summary>

Dragon can dictate into almost any text field. To do so:

1. Ensure the blinking text cursor is in the area you want to dictate text.
2. Activate Dragon.
3. Begin speaking.
4. Your text will be transcribed directly into the text field selected.

To turn Dragon on or off:

1. When the microphone icon is red, Dragon is not listening. Click the icon to start Dragon.
   ![The DragonBar with a red microphone button and the tooltip Currently off – click to turn on.](../assets/images/speech-to-text-tools/dragonbar-off.png) <!-- review-alt -->
2. When the microphone icon is green, Dragon is listening and will transcribe what you say.
   ![The DragonBar with a green microphone button and the tooltip Currently on – click to turn off.](../assets/images/speech-to-text-tools/dragonbar-on.png) <!-- review-alt -->
   1. Click the green icon to stop Dragon.
3. When dictating, say “go to sleep” and the icon will turn blue. Dragon is in sleep mode and won’t transcribe what you say. Say “wake up” or click the icon to continue using Dragon to transcribe your speech.
   ![The DragonBar with a blue sleeping microphone button and the tooltip Currently sleeping – click to wake up.](../assets/images/speech-to-text-tools/dragonbar-sleeping.png) <!-- review-alt -->

If Dragon makes a spelling error, say “correct that.” If the correct word appears in the menu, select it to automatically replace the incorrect word with the correct one. If the correct word does not appear in the menu, say “spell that” and spell the word letter by letter.

### Common Punctuation

- Say “period” or “comma” to add punctuation.
- Say “new line” to move to a new line.
- Say “delete that” to delete your last transcribed text or “delete last (number) words” to delete the last number of words that were transcribed.
- If Dragon makes a spelling error, say “correct that.” If the correct word appears in the menu, select it to automatically replace the incorrect word with the correct one. If the correct word does not appear in the menu, say “spell that” and spell the word letter by letter.
- However, it may be most efficient to use Dragon to dictate most of your answer and then make minor corrections using mouse and keyboard.

Dragon has a [YouTube playlist of videos](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6jzDdcZHxss&list=PLRFp5KqXtQ6zUuRgW4ewyYPDK82yVDfGG) demonstrating common features.

See [Voice Control](voice-control.md) for more on using Dragon.

</details>
